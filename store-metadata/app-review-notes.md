# Notes for App Review (V2.0)

Paste into App Store Connect → 2.0.0 → App Review Information → Notes.

```
GRIOT 2.0 adds Yorùbá language lessons to our existing translator.

No account or sign-in is needed.

PREMIUM / IN-APP PURCHASES
- Products are unchanged from previous versions (monthly, yearly, lifetime).
- The paywall appears after the first short lesson, on locked lessons (Unit 1 lessons 3–5 and all of Unit 2), on the Review tab after the first free review, and from "7 more languages with Premium" in the Translator tab.
- Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are on the paywall.
- Prices and any free trial are read from the App Store via RevenueCat; nothing is hard-coded.

HOW TO REACH THE MAIN FEATURES
1. Launch → Start learning → answer two questions → choose Yorùbá → complete the 2-minute first lesson (or tap "Can't speak right now").
2. Learn tab: lessons; Review tab: spaced-repetition review; Culture tab: proverb of the day, names; Translator tab: the original translator.
3. To skip learning entirely: "Skip to translator" on the first screen.

MICROPHONE
Used for voice translation and for recording pronunciation practice in lessons. Practice recordings stay on the device. Everything works without the microphone (typing in the translator; "Can't speak right now" in lessons).

NOTIFICATIONS
Local reminders only, off by default; enabled from the bell on the Learn tab. No push notifications.

ANALYTICS
Anonymous product analytics (PostHog, EU), no tracking, no advertising identifier. Can be turned off in Translator → Settings → Privacy.
```
