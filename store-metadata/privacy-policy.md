## Overview

GRIOT is a language-learning and translation app. We collect as little as possible:

- We do NOT collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number.
- We do NOT require an account.
- We do NOT upload or store your voice recordings on our servers.
- We do NOT track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use the advertising identifier.
- We do NOT sell your data, and the app contains no advertising.

## 1. Information we process

### 1.1 Voice (translator)
When you speak to the translator, your voice is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition. GRIOT does not store these recordings.

### 1.2 Practice recordings (lessons)
When you record yourself in a lesson, the recording is saved temporarily on your device so you can play it back and compare it with the native audio. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. The operating system clears these temporary files.

### 1.3 Translation text
Text you translate is sent to Google's translation service to produce the translation. Phrases that GRIOT's lessons already teach are answered from the app itself without being sent. We do not keep a history of your translations on any server.

### 1.4 Learning progress and settings (on your device)
Your onboarding answers (why you're learning, your level, your language), lesson progress, review schedule, streak, XP, daily goal and reminder time are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

### 1.5 Reminders
If you turn on reminders, GRIOT schedules notifications on your device at the time you choose. No push server is involved and no notification data is sent to us.

### 1.6 Anonymous usage data (analytics)
To understand which features help people learn, GRIOT sends a small set of anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider, on servers in the European Union. For example: the app was opened, a lesson was started or completed (with the lesson name and score), a daily goal was met, or the upgrade screen was viewed.

- Events are tied to a random identifier created for this installation only. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and it cannot identify you.
- We do not send what you say, type or translate, and we do not record your screen or taps.
- We do not use your IP address to work out your location. [check: "Discard client IP data" is turned on in PostHog]
- You can turn this off at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data**.

### 1.7 Purchases
Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. GRIOT never sees your payment details. RevenueCat manages subscription status using an anonymous app user ID and your App Store receipt.

### 1.8 What we don't collect
Name, email, phone number, contacts, photos (except a card you choose to save), precise or coarse location, device identifiers for tracking, health data, or payment card details.

## 2. How we use information
Only to provide the app's features (translation, lessons, reminders, Premium) and, through anonymous usage data, to improve the app. Never for advertising.

## 4. Data retention
Practice recordings, learning progress and settings stay on your device until you delete the app. Anonymous usage events are kept by PostHog for up to [check: your PostHog retention setting, e.g. 12 months] and then deleted. We keep no voice recordings or translation history.

## 5. Security
All network connections use encrypted HTTPS.

## 6. Your choices
- **Microphone:** used for voice translation and pronunciation practice. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings → GRIOT; you can still type translations and continue lessons.
- **Notifications:** off unless you turn on reminders; change them any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.
- **Analytics:** turn off in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.
- **Saving cards to Photos:** only when you choose "Save Image"; GRIOT asks permission first.
- **Your data:** because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

### Premium subscriptions
Premium is available as monthly or yearly subscriptions, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in your currency in the app before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

## 7. Children's privacy
GRIOT is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents may use GRIOT to learn alongside their children; GRIOT has no accounts, chat or advertising.
